Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

GTA 6 Clip Desk runs on your own computer. The author operates no server for it and does not collect, receive or store any personal data.

Data the app handles on your computer

  • Videos and clips you provide or create. They stay in the app's folder on your computer.
  • TikTok connection data, if you connect an account: an access token, a refresh token, your TikTok open ID and the granted permissions. These are stored in a local file readable only by your user account and are used only to talk to TikTok on your behalf.
  • TikTok account details shown in the app (username, display name and the privacy options available to you), fetched from TikTok when needed and not stored.

Data sent to others

  • TikTok: when you press the button to send a clip, that video file and the caption you wrote are sent to TikTok through its official API. TikTok handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • Video platforms: when you give the app a video link, it requests that video from the platform hosting it.

The app sends nothing to the author or to any analytics or advertising service.

Permissions requested from TikTok

  • video.upload: upload a clip to your TikTok inbox so you can finish the post in the TikTok app.
  • video.publish (optional): post a clip directly and read the posting options available to your account.

Removing your data

Press "Disconnect" in the app to revoke the TikTok token and delete it from your computer. You can also remove the app's access in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all videos and clips.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
